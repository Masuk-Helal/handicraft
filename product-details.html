Every bangle starts as a plain base that is wrapped by hand in fine silk thread, row by row, until the surface is smooth and even. Our artisans then stitch on mirrors, pearls, cowrie shells and sequins in traditional Rajasthani patterns.

The set is light enough to wear all day and pairs well with sarees, lehengas and kurtas. Store in the pouch provided and keep away from water and perfume to protect the thread's shine.
